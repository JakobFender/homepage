---
title: "Distribution Playground"
description: "An interactive web app for exploring 24 common probability distributions, from Normal and Beta to Poisson and Negative binomial, each in its own tab. You can adjust parameters with sliders, switch between PDF and CDF, and overlay random samples. A built-in Fit game hides a target distribution and challenges you to match it by tuning parameters (and, on hard mode, picking the family), scored by how much probability mass the two distributions share."
demoLink: "https://distribution-playground.jakob-fender.ch/"
